Help

Business math in plain English.

Cost

The money required to make a product or complete a job.

Profit

Selling price minus the costs included in your calculation.

Profit Margin

The percentage of the selling price left after the included costs.

Markup

The percentage added to cost. Markup and margin are not the same thing.

Labor

Your time is a business cost. Leaving labor blank means the calculation treats your time as costing $0.

Gross Profit vs Take-Home

Money remaining after the costs entered here is not automatically your final take-home income. Taxes, business expenses and other obligations may still apply.

Measurement Note

Weight and volume are different measurements. The toolkit will not pretend that ounces by weight equal fluid ounces. Converting between weight and volume requires the ingredient's density.
